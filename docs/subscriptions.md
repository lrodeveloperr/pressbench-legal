# Remove Ads Subscription, Cancellations, and Refunds

**Effective: 24 August 2026**

PressBench for Android is usable without a PressBench subscription. The optional **Remove Ads** subscription removes PressBench’s banner advertising while the subscription entitlement is active.

## Purchase terms

- Platform: Google Play.
- Product ID: `remove_ads_monthly`.
- Billing: auto-renewing monthly subscription.
- Benefit: removes PressBench banner ads while active.
- Core PressBench production functionality remains available without the subscription.
- The local price, currency, taxes where applicable, and billing terms are displayed by Google Play before purchase.
- No free trial or introductory offer applies unless Google Play explicitly displays one for your account and market at the time of purchase.

## Renewal and cancellation

The subscription renews automatically until cancelled. You can manage or cancel it from **PressBench → Settings → Manage subscription** when the entitlement is active, or directly through Google Play’s subscription controls.

Cancelling stops future renewal. Unless Google Play or applicable law provides otherwise, access to the ad-free entitlement normally continues until the end of the already-paid billing period.

Uninstalling PressBench or deleting local PressBench data does **not** cancel the subscription.

## Restore

Use **Settings → Restore Purchases** if PressBench does not recognise an active Google Play entitlement. Restoration does not create a new charge.

## Refunds

Refund eligibility and processing depend on Google Play rules and applicable consumer law. Where Google Play offers a refund route, use Google Play’s purchase controls. You may also contact us for assistance identifying the relevant purchase route. We do not need or want your payment-card details or purchase token by email.

## Price changes

Google Play controls storefront pricing and applicable purchase notices. If the subscription price changes, Google Play will provide any notice or consent process required by its rules or applicable law.
