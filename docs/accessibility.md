# Accessibility Statement

> **Pre-release draft.** Accessibility claims must be tested in the final native builds and exported documents before release.

**Last updated: 10 August 2026**

GoodUse Studios aims to make PressBench and these support pages usable by people with a range of abilities, devices, display sizes, and input methods.

The audited App source is designed to include semantic labels for many controls, visible keyboard focus treatment, scalable layouts, light and dark appearance options, minimum-size touch targets, status text in addition to colour, and multiple interface languages. These features do not constitute a claim that every screen or exported document has been independently certified against WCAG or another standard.

Known areas requiring continued testing include screen-reader order in complex sheets, long translated text, high text-scaling levels, chart alternatives, keyboard-only use, reduced motion, and accessibility of PDF and XLSX exports.

To report an accessibility barrier, email [lrodeveloperr@gmail.com](mailto:lrodeveloperr@gmail.com?subject=PressBench%20Accessibility) with the subject **“PressBench Accessibility.”** Include the platform, App version, assistive technology, screen, and the result you need. Do not include confidential production data.
