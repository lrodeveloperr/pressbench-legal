# Accessibility Statement

> **Pre-release draft.** Accessibility claims must be tested in the final Android and iOS interfaces and exported documents before release.

**Last updated: 12 August 2026**

GoodUse Studios aims to make PressBench and its legal and support information usable by people with a range of abilities, devices, display sizes, languages, and input methods.

The v0.21.2 artifact reviewed for the legal pack defines application logic rather than a final user interface. It therefore does not establish conformance with WCAG, Apple accessibility guidance, Android accessibility guidance, or another standard.

Before publication, the final applications should be tested for:

- screen-reader names, roles, states, reading order, and error announcements;
- text scaling, reflow, truncation, and long translated content;
- touch-target size and spacing;
- keyboard, switch, and alternative-input operation where supported;
- contrast, focus indication, and information conveyed without colour alone;
- reduced-motion and animation settings;
- timer and notification alternatives;
- chart and analytics text alternatives; and
- accessibility of PDF and XLSX exports.

No conformance or certification claim should be made until supported by completed testing.

To report an accessibility barrier, email [lrodeveloperr@gmail.com](mailto:lrodeveloperr@gmail.com?subject=PressBench%20Accessibility) with the subject **“PressBench Accessibility.”** Include the platform, App version, assistive technology, affected action, and the result needed. Do not include confidential production data.
