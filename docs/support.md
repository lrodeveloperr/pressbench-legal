# PressBench Support

> **Pre-release draft.** Support procedures must be verified against the released Android and iOS applications before launch.

**Last updated: 12 August 2026**

Email: [lrodeveloperr@gmail.com](mailto:lrodeveloperr@gmail.com?subject=PressBench%20Support)  
Provider: **Lateef Razaq-Oyetola carrying on business as GoodUse Studios**, Ontario, Canada

## What to include

Use the subject **“PressBench Support”** and include only what is necessary:

- Android or iOS;
- device model and operating-system version;
- PressBench version;
- the action involved;
- what you expected and what happened; and
- exact error text, if any.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, purchase tokens, JSON backups, customer or job names, confidential production records, or complete reports. Redact unrelated information from screenshots and store references.

## Local-data limitation

PressBench has no GoodUse Studios cloud account or operational database. Support cannot access, retrieve, edit, or restore records stored only on a device.

Use the App’s backup function and verify important exported files. Purchase restoration restores only an eligible store entitlement; it does not restore operational records.

## Purchases

For purchase restoration and refund information, see [Purchases, Paid Access, and Refunds]({{ '/purchases/' | relative_url }}).

PressBench has no subscription or recurring charge. There is nothing to cancel after the one-time purchase. To troubleshoot an entitlement, use the subject **“PressBench Purchase”** and include only a redacted store order or transaction reference if necessary.

## Privacy and deletion requests

For personal information GoodUse Studios actually holds through support, use the subject **“PressBench Privacy.”** We may need enough information to find the correspondence and verify the request.

We cannot process a remote access or deletion request for operational records stored only on the device because we do not possess them. See [Local Data, Backup, Export, and Deletion]({{ '/data-choices/' | relative_url }}).

## Safety

Support does not provide heat-press operating, engineering, calibration, material-compatibility, workplace-safety, or emergency advice. Use current manufacturer and supplier instructions. For an emergency, follow workplace procedures and contact local emergency services.
