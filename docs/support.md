# PressBench Support

> **Pre-release draft.** Support procedures must be verified against the released Android and iOS builds before launch.

**Last updated: 10 August 2026**

## What to include

Use the subject **“PressBench Support”** and include:

- Android or iOS;
- device model and operating-system version;
- PressBench version;
- the screen and action involved;
- what you expected and what happened; and
- exact error text, if any.

Do not send passwords, full payment-card details, purchase tokens, JSON backups, customer names, supplier-confidential data, or complete production reports. If a screenshot is necessary, redact unrelated production information first.

## Local-data limitation

PressBench has no cloud account or remote database. Support cannot access or recover records stored only on a lost, reset, damaged, uninstalled, or cleared device. Use **Data Management → Backup & restore** regularly and verify important exported files.

## Purchases

For cancellation, restoration, refund, and billing instructions, see [Purchases, Pro Access, Cancellations, and Refunds]({{ '/subscriptions/' | relative_url }}). For Android developer-refund consideration, use the subject **“PressBench Android Refund Review”** and include the Google Play order ID, purchase date, country storefront, App version, and reason. Never email passwords, full payment-card information, purchase tokens, JSON backups, or confidential production records.

## Safety

Support does not provide heat-press operating, engineering, material-compatibility, workplace-safety, or emergency advice. Consult the current manufacturer and material supplier instructions. For an emergency, follow workplace procedures and contact local emergency services.
