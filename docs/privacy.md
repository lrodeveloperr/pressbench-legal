# Privacy Policy

**Effective: 28 August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **PressBench** (the “App”) handles information. PressBench is provided by **Lateef Razaq-Oyetola carrying on business as GoodUse Studios, Ontario, Canada** (“GoodUse Studios,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

## 1. Summary

PressBench is a local-first heat-press production log.

- No PressBench account or login is required to use the App.
- On iOS, optional Sign in with Apple can enable automatic backup to the user’s private iCloud storage. Continuing without signing in remains available.
- GoodUse Studios does not operate or receive data through a developer-controlled production database or synchronization server.
- Operational records and preferences are stored in the App’s private storage on the device and, only when the iOS user enables Apple backup, in that user’s private iCloud container.
- You can deliberately export a production report and choose where to share it using the platform’s system share sheet.
- The closed-test build uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform (“UMP”) and may display Google test banner ads. Ads are requested as non-personalized ads.
- PressBench does not include a GoodUse Studios analytics, crash-reporting or attribution service.
- Android application backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled and all supported storage domains are explicitly excluded. The optional iOS iCloud backup is a separate, user-enabled feature.
- We do not sell or rent your production records.

## 2. Information stored locally

Depending on how you use PressBench, local records may include:

- machine names, makes, models and platen sizes;
- setup names, materials and transfer labels;
- temperature, time, pressure, pre-press, peel and second-press details;
- job references and planned quantities;
- active-run state, timer state, first-piece attempts and quality checks;
- processed, first-pass, reworked and wasted quantities, notes and timestamps; and
- preferences such as language, temperature unit, sound, vibration and onboarding state.

Some fields are free text. Do not enter personal, customer, employee, confidential or trade-secret information unless it is necessary and you are authorized to store it on the device.

GoodUse Studios does not receive these production records during ordinary App operation and cannot see, recover or remotely delete them. When optional iOS backup is enabled, Apple stores the backup in the user’s private iCloud container; GoodUse Studios does not receive a copy.

## 3. Optional Sign in with Apple and iCloud backup on iOS

On iOS, Sign in with Apple is optional and is used only to enable the App’s Apple backup controls. PressBench does not request the user’s name or email address. The stable Apple authorization identifier is kept in the device Keychain so the App can verify whether authorization remains valid; it is not sent to GoodUse Studios.

When enabled, PressBench automatically writes a portable backup of machines, setups, completed runs and App settings to the App’s private iCloud Drive container. The backup excludes App Store purchase entitlement and active-run session state. Apple processes the authorization and iCloud data under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. The iCloud account used by the device and Apple’s iCloud availability determine whether backup and restore can operate.

Users can back up immediately, restore from iCloud after an explicit overwrite warning, turn off automatic backup, and permanently delete the iCloud backup in Settings. Turning off backup does not by itself delete the existing iCloud copy. Deleting the iCloud backup does not delete data stored locally on the device.

## 4. Google advertising and consent services

The closed-test build includes **Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.4.0** and **Google UMP SDK 4.0.0**. It uses Google’s official test app and banner identifiers, so displayed banners are test ads and do not generate advertising revenue. Before requesting an ad, the App asks UMP for the applicable consent status and displays a Google consent form when Google determines that one is required. A privacy-options control is shown when UMP reports that it is required.

PressBench adds Google’s non-personalized-ad signal to each banner request. “Non-personalized” does not mean that no technical data is processed. According to Google’s Mobile Ads SDK disclosures, Google may automatically collect or receive:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
- app and ad interactions, such as app launches, ad views and taps;
- diagnostic information, such as performance, launch time, hangs and energy use; and
- device or account identifiers, such as the advertising ID, app set ID and related identifiers available to the SDK.

Google states that this information may be used for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention and is encrypted in transit. Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy policies. See:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK Play data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)

The App does not send your machine, setup, run, report or free-text production fields to Google Mobile Ads.

## 5. Advertising and billing status

The reviewed closed-test build may show Google test banner ads. All implemented App features are available without payment. The build has no Google Play Billing dependency, paid product, purchase interface or subscription controls, and cannot create a charge, renewal, restoration or entitlement.

Any later build that introduces a paid product or production ad unit will require an updated policy, store declaration and applicable purchase terms before distribution.

## 6. PDF and CSV exports

When you choose an export, PressBench creates the report locally and opens the platform’s system share sheet. Android supports PDF/CSV export; iOS supports PDF/XLSX export. The App does not choose the recipient or upload the report automatically. The destination you select—such as email, messaging, cloud storage or another app—receives the report and handles it under that provider’s privacy practices.

Exports may contain the setup, machine, date, quantities, calculated first-pass yield, rework, waste and notes shown in the report. Review an export before sharing it. Temporary cache files may remain until Android or the App clears them.

## 7. External links and support communications

Legal and support links open in an external browser or email app. Those providers may process ordinary connection, device, browser, email, cookie and security information under their own policies.

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include. We use support communications to answer the request, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, protect legal rights and meet legal obligations. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, purchase tokens, full production databases, customer information or confidential supplier information.

We generally retain ordinary support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary and ordinarily no longer than 24 months after the last substantive interaction, unless a longer period is required for security, accounting, dispute resolution or law.

## 8. Legal and support website

The PressBench legal and support pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. GoodUse Studios does not intentionally add advertising or marketing analytics to those pages. GitHub may process technical request, security, device/browser, log and cookie information under GitHub’s own privacy practices.

## 9. Retention, deletion and control

Local PressBench records and preferences remain on the device until you delete them in PressBench, clear the App’s storage, uninstall the App or the operating system removes them. PressBench sets Android backup off and supplies explicit rules excluding every supported storage domain from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

On iOS, an optional iCloud backup remains until it is replaced or the user chooses **Settings → Apple & iCloud Backup → Delete iCloud backup**. Turning off backup stops future automatic backups but leaves the existing iCloud copy available. Apple may also provide iCloud storage controls at the operating-system or account level.

Use **Settings → Delete Local Data** and confirm the deletion to clear PressBench’s local records and preferences on that device. On iOS this also turns off automatic PressBench backup so the retained iCloud copy is not immediately overwritten by the empty local state. GoodUse Studios cannot remotely retrieve or delete local or iCloud records it never received.

Deleting local App data does not delete:

- a report or backup file that you already shared or saved elsewhere;
- an optional PressBench iCloud backup, unless you separately delete it using the iOS backup controls;
- support email already sent to GoodUse Studios; or
- information independently processed by Google Play, Google Mobile Ads, UMP, the device platform, a browser, an email provider or GitHub Pages.

Where UMP provides privacy choices, use the App’s **Privacy choices** control. You may also reset the App’s storage, subject to applicable law and Google’s own controls.

## 10. International processing and service providers

Apple (for optional Sign in with Apple, Keychain and iCloud backup), Google Play, Google Mobile Ads, UMP, GitHub Pages, email providers, device platforms and any destination you select for an export may process information in more than one country under their own terms and privacy documentation. GoodUse Studios does not automatically receive your local production database from these services.

## 11. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information that GoodUse Studios actually holds, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, to withdraw consent where consent is the basis, or to complain to a privacy regulator. These rights are subject to applicable law and exceptions.

Because GoodUse Studios does not receive the local or optional iCloud production database during ordinary App operation, requests to us normally concern support correspondence or other information we actually hold. For local production data and optional iCloud backup, use the controls in the App and on your device.

## 12. Children

PressBench is a professional heat-press workflow utility intended only for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. Google Play is configured to restrict users it determines to be minors from finding or downloading the App, but this control may not identify every minor. Do not use the App if you are under 18, below the age of legal majority where you live, or otherwise do not meet the eligibility requirements in the Terms of Use.

## 13. Security

The Android App uses HTTPS-capable Google SDKs and Android’s private app storage, disables clear-text network traffic, disables Android application backup and explicitly excludes App data from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Information handled by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit according to Google. On iOS, the optional Apple authorization identifier is retained in the device Keychain and backup data is written only to the App’s private iCloud container using Apple platform services. No device or software is completely secure, so keep your device updated, use appropriate access controls and avoid entering unnecessary personal or confidential information.
