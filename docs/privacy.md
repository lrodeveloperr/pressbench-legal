# Privacy Policy

**Effective: 25 August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **PressBench** (the “App”) handles information. PressBench is provided by **Lateef Razaq-Oyetola carrying on business as GoodUse Studios, Ontario, Canada** (“GoodUse Studios,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

## 1. Privacy summary

PressBench is a local-first heat-press production workbench.

- No PressBench account or login.
- No GoodUse Studios cloud database or synchronization for production records.
- Machine, setup, active-run, timer, quality-check and completed-run records are stored in the App’s local WebView storage on the device.
- The reviewed Android build does not implement camera capture, photo storage, cloud sync or export, and does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or Google Play Billing SDKs.
- No microphone, camera, contacts, location, Bluetooth or broad shared-storage permission is requested.
- Android application backup is disabled.
- We do not sell or rent your production records.

The current closed-test build contains visual advertising space and purchase/privacy-choice controls for interface inspection. They are not connected to Google Mobile Ads, Google UMP or Google Play Billing and do not request ads, obtain advertising consent or process purchases. This policy must be reviewed again before any release that activates advertising, consent or billing.

## 2. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use PressBench, local records may include:

- machine nicknames and machine details;
- setup names, materials and stored transfer labels;
- temperature, time, pressure, pre-press, peel and second-press details;
- job references and planned quantities;
- active-run state, timer state, first-piece attempts and quality-check status;
- processed, good, reworked and wasted quantities, notes and timestamps; and
- local preferences such as language, temperature unit, sound, vibration and onboarding state.

Some fields are free text. Do not enter personal, customer, employee, confidential or trade-secret information unless it is necessary and you are authorized to store it on the device.

GoodUse Studios does not operate a server that receives these production records during ordinary App operation and cannot see, recover or remotely delete them.

## 3. Network access and external links

The manifest currently includes `INTERNET`, although the reviewed build does not itself make an operational network request. The operational interface is bundled with the installation, and its core recordkeeping workflow works without a PressBench account or GoodUse Studios server. Legal and support links are handed to an external browser or email app.

When you deliberately open one of those links, the destination provider may process ordinary connection, device, browser, email and security information under its own privacy policy. GoodUse Studios does not control those providers.

The reviewed build does not automatically upload production records to GoodUse Studios or another service.

## 4. Advertising, consent and billing

The reviewed Android build does not include Google Mobile Ads, Google UMP or Google Play Billing code. It therefore does not serve Google ads, request advertising consent, verify a subscription or process an in-app purchase.

The visible banner reservation, “Privacy choices,” “Remove Ads,” “Restore purchases” and “Manage subscription” controls in this closed-test build are non-functional inspection placeholders. They must not be treated as evidence that an advertising or subscription service is active.

If a later production build adds advertising, consent or billing, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety and advertising declarations before distribution of that build.

## 5. Reports and exports

The reviewed build can display local run history and production summaries. Its PDF and CSV buttons are non-functional inspection placeholders and do not currently create, save, upload or share a file.

If a later build enables export or sharing, the policy will be updated to describe the exact formats, data included and destination flow before that feature is released.

## 6. Support communications

If you choose to contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use support communications to answer the request, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, protect legal rights and meet legal obligations.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, purchase tokens, full production databases, customer information or confidential supplier information. We generally retain ordinary support correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed for support and recordkeeping and ordinarily no longer than 24 months after the last substantive interaction, unless a longer period is required for security, accounting, dispute resolution or law.

## 7. Legal and support website

The PressBench legal and support pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. GoodUse Studios does not intentionally add advertising or marketing analytics to those pages. GitHub may process technical request, security, device/browser, log and cookie information under GitHub’s own privacy practices.

## 8. Retention, deletion and control

Local PressBench records and preferences remain on the device until you delete them in PressBench, clear the App’s storage, uninstall the App or the operating system removes them. The Android release disables Android application backup for PressBench.

Use **Settings → Delete Local Data** and confirm the deletion to clear PressBench keys from the App’s local storage on that device. GoodUse Studios cannot remotely retrieve or delete local records that it never received.

Deleting local App data does not delete support email already sent to GoodUse Studios or information processed independently by Google Play, the device platform, the browser, the email provider or GitHub Pages.

## 9. International processing and service providers

Google Play, GitHub Pages, email providers, device platforms and any browser or email app you choose may process information in more than one country under their own terms and privacy documentation. The reviewed build does not send production records to those providers automatically.

## 10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information that GoodUse Studios actually holds, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, to withdraw consent where consent is the basis, or to complain to a privacy regulator. These rights are subject to applicable law and exceptions.

Because GoodUse Studios does not receive the local production database during ordinary App operation, requests to us normally concern support correspondence or other information actually held by us. For local production data, use the controls on your device.

## 11. Children

PressBench is a professional heat-press workflow utility intended for adults and is not directed to children. Do not use the App if you do not meet the eligibility requirements in the Terms of Use.

## 12. Security

PressBench’s local-first design limits remote exposure of production records, but no device or software is completely secure. Keep your device and operating system updated, use appropriate device access controls and avoid entering unnecessary personal or confidential information.

## 13. Changes

We may update this policy when the App, service providers or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will be posted here. Where required, additional notice or consent will be provided before a material change takes effect.
