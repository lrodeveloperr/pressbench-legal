# PressBench Legal & Support

> **Pre-release draft — not yet effective.** The platform offer structure is fixed, but the prices, Android billing period and recurring value, native purchase implementation, and signed app binaries still require approval and verification.

PressBench is a local-first production settings, batch-record, analytics, and report-export utility for heat-press operators. It records information entered by the operator; it does not inspect or control heat-press equipment and does not determine safe or correct operating settings.
