# PressBench Legal & Support

> **Pre-release draft — not yet effective.** These pages are based on PressBench logic v0.21.2. The final signed applications and store configuration remain subject to release verification.

PressBench is a local-first setup, run, batch-record, analytics, and export utility for heat-press operators. It records operator-entered information; it does not inspect, measure, monitor, or control heat-press equipment and does not determine safe or correct operating settings.
